# 1 Timothy 4:10 — toil and reproach

The Thai reads **ทำงานหนักและถูกกล่าวร้าย** (“we toil and are reviled”). SBLGNT prints κοπιῶμεν καὶ ὀνειδιζόμεθα. The first verb describes the workers’ effort. The passive second verb describes the reproach they receive.

NA28 prints ἀγωνιζόμεθα (“we strive”) in the second position. The verse note records this variant.
