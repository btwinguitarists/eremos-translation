# 2 Timothy 2:14 — Lord or God

The Thai uses องค์พระผู้เป็นเจ้า for κυρίου in the charge to Timothy. The SBLGNT prints ἐνώπιον τοῦ κυρίου. Its apparatus gives the same reading for Robinson–Pierpont and records θεοῦ in Westcott–Hort, Tregelles and NA28.

The textual note in the NET Bible identifies A, D, Ψ and 048 among the witnesses for κυρίου. It records θεοῦ in א, C, F, G and I, among others. It also identifies a third reading, Χριστοῦ, in 206, 429 and 1758. These examples describe the distribution and do not determine which wording Eremos uses.

The two principal readings identify the divine witness as the Lord or God. Eremos follows its stated Greek base here. The difference is a substitution, so the Thai has no inclusion brackets. The running verse note identifies the alternative reading.

Sources: SBL Greek New Testament apparatus at 2 Timothy 2:14 and NET Bible textual note at 2 Timothy 2:14.
