## Chapter intro

# Isaiah 65 General Notes

## Structure and Formatting

Some translations set each line of poetry farther to the right than the rest of the text to make it easier to read. The ULT does this with the poetry in this chapter.

This chapter is Yahweh’s response to the repentance of the people. (See: [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/repent]])

## Religious and Cultural Concepts in This Chapter

### Judgment and hope

This chapter records that Yahweh’s judgment on the people must come. Despite this, there is hope that in the end, Yahweh will make all things new and restore them to himself. (See: [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/judge]] and [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/hope]] and [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/restore]])

## Isaiah 65:1

**Quote:** I was ready
Here “I” refers to Yahweh.

---

## Isaiah 65:2

**Quote:** I have spread out my hands all day to a stubborn people
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-idiom
“Spreading out my hands” here is a person’s gesture to show imploring or a strong request. Alternate translation: [I have continually begged a stubborn people to receive my help]

---

## Isaiah 65:3

**Quote:** in gardens … on brick tiles
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-explicit
These refer to Canaanite places for worshiping idols. Their sacred altars were made of bricks, which Yahweh forbid for his altars. Yahweh’s altars were made of stone.

---

## Isaiah 65:4

**Quote:** sit among the graves and keep watch all night
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-explicit
This is a reference to consulting the dead, a practice that Yahweh prohibited.

**Quote:** eat pork
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-explicit
Yahweh did not allow the people of Israel to eat meat from pigs.

---

## Isaiah 65:5

**Quote:** These things are smoke in my nose
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Yahweh compares these people who are continually annoying him to smoke that irritates a person’s breathing.

**Quote:** a fire that burns all day long
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Yahweh compares the people of Israel to a slow-burning fire that sends out smoke without stopping as it irritates.

---

## Isaiah 65:6

**Quote:** Look, it is written
Alternate translation: [Take notice and pay attention]

**Quote:** into their laps
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-idiom
This means God will punish them to the fullest extent. This expression compares Yahweh punishing the people to dumping on their legs as they are sitting. (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor]])

---

## Isaiah 65:8

**Quote:** As when juice is found in a cluster of grapes
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Yahweh compares the people of Israel to a cluster of grapes that still has some good juice in it.

**Quote:** when juice is found in a cluster
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-activepassive
This can be stated in an active way. Alternate translation: [when you find juice in a cluster]

**Quote:** I will not ruin them all
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-litotes
This can be expressed positively. Alternate translation: [I will spare some of them who are righteous] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-explicit]])

---

## Isaiah 65:9

**Quote:** my mountains
This refers to the highest locations of Jerusalem and all of Judea.

---

## Isaiah 65:10

**Quote:** Sharon
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/translate-names
This was a fertile grazing area.

**Quote:** Valley of Achor
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/translate-names
This is the name of a valley that possibly ran from Jerusalem to south of Jericho. This was also a fertile grazing area.

---

## Isaiah 65:11

**Quote:** holy mountain
See how you translated this in [Isaiah 11:9](../11/09.md).

**Quote:** who prepare a table … and fill wine glasses of mixed wine
People would bring food and drink and place it in front of the idol as part of their worship.

**Quote:** mixed wine
wine mixed with spices

**Quote:** Fortune … Destiny
These are names of false gods. They are also called “Gad” and “Meni.”

---

## Isaiah 65:12

**Quote:** appoint you for the sword
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-synecdoche
“The sword” represents various weapons of war that Yahweh will use to punish those who do not respond to Yahweh’s call.

**Quote:** when I called, you did not answer; when I spoke, you did not listen
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-parallelism
Both clauses mean the same and are repeated for emphasis.

---

## Isaiah 65:13

**Quote:** Look, my servants
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-parallelism
“Take notice and pay attention” Yahweh repeats this for emphasis.

---

## Isaiah 65:14

**Quote:** but you will cry because of the pain of the heart, and will wail because of the crushing of the spirit
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-parallelism
These clauses mean the same and the repetition is for emphasis.

**Quote:** crushing of the spirit
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
This expression compares the feeling of terrible disappointment and sorrow to something becoming deformed due to high pressure.

---

## Isaiah 65:16

**Quote:** will be blessed by me, the God of truth
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-activepassive
If your language does not use the passive form in this way, you can state this in active form or in another way that is natural in your language. Alternate translation: [I, the God who always speaks the truth, will bless him]

**Quote:** the former troubles will be forgotten … will be hidden
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-activepassive
These clauses can be stated in active form. Alternate translation: [they will forget the former troubles, for these troubles will be out of my memory]

**Quote:** they will be hidden from my eyes
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-idiom
“hidden from my eyes” represents the attention and memory of Yahweh. Alternate translation: [I will not even think about them again] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-personification]])

## Isaiah 65:17

**Quote:** For see
Alternate translation: [Notice! Pay attention!]

**Quote:** new heavens and a new earth
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-merism
Both are extremes that also represent everything between.

**Quote:** the former things will not be remembered or be brought to mind
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-parallelism
Both of these clauses mean the same thing and are combined for emphasis. If your language does not use the passive form in this way, you can state this in active form or in another way that is natural in your language. Alternate translation: [you will not even think about what happened in the past] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-activepassive]])
