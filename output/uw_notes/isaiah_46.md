## Chapter intro

# Isaiah 46 General Notes

## Structure and Formatting

Some translations set each line of poetry farther to the right than the rest of the text to make it easier to read. The ULT does this with the poetry in this chapter.

### Yahweh and the false gods

This chapter is intended to compare Yahweh with various false Gods whom the Israelites worship. There really is no comparison at all because Yahweh alone is God. (See: [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/falsegod]])

---

## Isaiah 46:1

**Quote:** Bel bows down, Nebo stoops; their idols … for weary animals
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Isaiah speaks of people placing the idols of Bel and Nebo into a cart for animals to transport as if these gods were made to “bow down” and “stoop.” These are both postures of humiliation.

**Quote:** Bel … Nebo
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/translate-names
These were the two primary gods whom the Babylonians worshiped.

**Quote:** their idols
the idols that represented Bel and Nebo

---

## Isaiah 46:2

**Quote:** they cannot rescue the images
Alternate translation: [Bel and Nebo cannot rescue their images]

**Quote:** they themselves have gone off into captivity
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Isaiah speaks of people carrying off these idols as if the false gods whom they represent are being carried off to captivity.

---

## Isaiah 46:3

**Quote:** Listen to me
Here “me” refers to Yahweh.

**Quote:** who have been carried by me from before your birth, carried from the womb
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Yahweh speaks of the nation of Israel as if it were a person, and of the nation’s beginning as if it were its birth.

**Quote:** who have been carried by me
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Yahweh speaks of helping and rescuing the people of Israel as if he were carrying them. If your language does not use the passive form in this way, you can state this in active form or in another way that is natural in your language. Alternate translation: [whom I have carried] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-activepassive]])

---

## Isaiah 46:4

**Quote:** Even to your old age I am he, and until your hair is gray I will carry you
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Yahweh speaks of the nation of Israel becoming very old as if it were an old man with gray hair.

---

## Isaiah 46:5

**Quote:** To whom will you compare me? Who do you think I resemble, so that we may be compared?
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-rquestion
Yahweh uses questions to emphasize that there is no one like him. Alternate translation: [There is no one to whom you can compare me. I resemble no one, so that we might be compared.]

**Quote:** so that we may be compared
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-activepassive
If your language does not use the passive form in this way, you can state this in active form or in another way that is natural in your language. Alternate translation: [so that you may compare us]

---

## Isaiah 46:7

**Quote:** They lift it
“They” refers to the people who make idols and “it” refers to the idol that they have created.

---

## Isaiah 46:10

**Quote:** I announce the end from the beginning, and beforehand what has not yet happened
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-parallelism
This basically repeats the same idea for emphasis. The verb from the first phrase may be supplied for the second. Alternate translation: [I announce the end from the beginning, and I announce beforehand what has not yet happened] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-ellipsis]])

---

## Isaiah 46:11

**Quote:** I call a bird of prey from the east
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Yahweh speaks of Cyrus as if he were “a bird of prey.” As a bird swiftly captures its prey, so Cyrus will swiftly accomplish Yahweh’s purpose to conquer the nations.

**Quote:** I have spoken; I will also accomplish it; I have purposed, I will also do it.
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-parallelism
This repeats the same idea for emphasis.

---

## Isaiah 46:12

**Quote:** who are far from doing what is right
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Yahweh speaks of the people stubbornly doing wrong as if they were physically far away from doing the right thing.

---

## Isaiah 46:13

**Quote:** my salvation does not wait
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-personification
Yahweh speaks of saving his people soon as if his salvation were a person who does not wait to act. If your language does not use an abstract noun for the idea behind the word **salvation**, you can express the same idea with a verb. Alternate translation: [I will not wait to save you] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-abstractnouns]])
