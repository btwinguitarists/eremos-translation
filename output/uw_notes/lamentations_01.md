## Chapter intro

# Lamentations 1 Introduction

## Structure and Formatting

This chapter is a lament [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/lament]] over the destruction of Jerusalem and the exile [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/other/exile]] of Judah’s people to Babylon. The narrator and personified Jerusalem alternate as speakers, mourning her desolation.

## Religious and Cultural Concepts in This Chapter

### Jerusalem Personified as a Woman

Throughout this chapter, Jerusalem is addressed as a woman — a widow, a princess, a grieving mother. Translators should use feminine pronouns consistently for the city.

## Translation Issues in This Chapter

### Acrostic Structure

Each of the 22 verses begins with a successive letter of the Hebrew alphabet. This acrostic cannot be reproduced in translation; a footnote may inform readers of this literary feature.
