## Chapter intro

# Ecclesiastes 3 General Notes

## Structure and Formatting

Some translations set each line of poetry farther to the right than the rest of the text to make it easier to read. The ULT does this with the poetry in 3:1–8 and [3:15](../03/15.md).

## Translation Issues in This Chapter

### Parallelism

The chapter uses parallelism with the phrase, “a time to.” This gives the quotation a poetic style. Their overall purpose is to show that Yahweh directs the events of the world and therefore, they have purpose.

## Ecclesiastes 3:11

**Quote:** God has made everything suitable for its own time
“God has fixed a time that is right for everything to happen” or “God has set a time that is right for each thing to happen”

**Quote:** placed eternity in their hearts
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metonymy
Here the word “their” refers to human beings. Here the “hearts” of the people represent their thoughts and desires. Alternate translation: [placed eternity in the hearts of human beings] or [caused people to think about eternal things]

**Quote:** from their beginning all the way to their end
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-merism
This refers to the beginning and the end and all that is in between.

## Ecclesiastes 3:19

**Quote:** is the same fate for them
Alternate translation: [is the same for both of them] or [is the same]

**Quote:** The breath is the same for all of them
Alternate translation: [All of them breathe same]

**Quote:** There is no advantage for mankind over the animals
Alternate translation: [Mankind is no better off than the animals]

**Quote:** is not everything just a breath?
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Here the author speaks of everything being as temporary as if it were a breath of air. The author uses this rhetorical question to emphasize that everything is temporary. If it would be helpful in your language, you could express this question as a statement. Alternate translation: [Everything is just a breath.] or [Everything is as temporary as a breath.] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-rquestion]])

**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-rquestion
