## Chapter intro

# Ezekiel 47 General Notes

## Structure and Formatting

Instructions for the new temple and city continue in this chapter. There was a river flowing from the temple. The land was to be divided among the tribes. The landscape of Jerusalem will have changed in a significant way. (See: [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/temple]])

---

## Ezekiel 47:1

**Quote:** to the right of the altar
This is the right side of the altar when a person looks at it while they are facing east, so it is on the south side of the altar. Alternate translation: [on the south side of the altar]

---

## Ezekiel 47:2

**Quote:** the gate facing east
Alternate translation: [the eastern gate] or [the eastern gate of the outer wall]

---

## Ezekiel 47:3

**Quote:** measuring line
a string or a rope that people use to measure longer distances

**Quote:** one thousand cubits
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/translate-bdistance
Each long cubit was about 54 centimeters. Alternate translation: [1,000 cubits] or [about 540 meters]

---

## Ezekiel 47:5

**Quote:** a river that could not be crossed
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-activepassive
This can be translated in active form. Alternate translation: [a river that no one could cross on foot]

---

## Ezekiel 47:6

**Quote:** Son of man
God calls Ezekiel this to emphasize that Ezekiel is only a human being. God is eternal and powerful, but humans are not. See how you translated this in [Ezekiel 2:1](../02/01.md). Alternate translation: [Mortal person] or [Human]

**Quote:** do you see this?
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-rquestion
Yahweh is commanding Ezekiel to think about what he has just seen. See how you translated this in [Ezekiel 8:15](../08/15.md). Alternate translation: [think about this.]
