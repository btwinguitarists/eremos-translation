## Chapter intro

# Lamentations 5 Introduction

## Structure and Formatting

This chapter is a communal prayer addressed directly to [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/yahweh]]. Unlike the four preceding chapters, it is not an acrostic: it has 22 verses matching the Hebrew alphabet count, but the verses do not follow alphabetical order.

## Religious and Cultural Concepts in This Chapter

### Orphans and Widows

The community describes itself as fatherless and its mothers as [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/other/widow]] (verse 3), the most vulnerable members of ancient society. This imagery conveys total helplessness and loss of protection.

### Yahweh's Eternal Throne

The chapter closes with an appeal to Yahweh's everlasting kingship (verse 19) and a plea for [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/restore]] (verse 21), expressing hope that his permanent reign means he can yet act on their behalf.
