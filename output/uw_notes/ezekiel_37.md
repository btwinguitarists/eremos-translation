## Chapter intro

# Ezekiel 37 General Notes

## Religious and Cultural Concepts in This Chapter

### Israel restored

God will unite the two kingdoms of Israel and Judah and the Messiah will rule over them. This will come in a prophesied day of restoration. (See: [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/christ]] and [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/restore]])

## Translation Issues in This Chapter

### Metaphor

The people are discouraged and compare themselves to skeletons, but God will cause them to become a nation again. (See: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor]])

## Ezekiel 37:1

# General Information:

Ezekiel is speaking.

**Quote:** The hand of Yahweh was upon me
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metonymy
The word “hand” is often used to refer to someone’s power or action. A person with his hand on another person has power over that other person. See how you translated a similar phrase in [Ezekiel 1:3](../01/03.md). Alternate translation: [Yahweh was controlling me]

**Quote:** in the midst
Alternate translation: [in the middle]

---

## Ezekiel 37:2

**Quote:** round and round
Alternate translation: [in every direction]

**Quote:** Behold!
The word “Behold” here alerts us to pay attention to the surprising information that follows.

---

## Ezekiel 37:3

**Quote:** Son of man
God calls Ezekiel this to emphasize that Ezekiel is only a human being. God is eternal and powerful, but humans are not. See how you translated this in [Ezekiel 2:1](../02/01.md). Alternate translation: [Mortal person] or [Human]

## Ezekiel 37:4

# General Information:

**Quote:** Listen to the word of Yahweh
Alternate translation: [Listen to Yahweh’s message]

## Ezekiel 37:5

**Quote:** Behold
Alternate translation: [Look] or [Listen] or [Pay attention to what I am about to tell you]

**Quote:** to put breath into you
The noun “breath” can be stated as the verb “breathe.” Alternate translation: [I will cause you to breathe]

**Quote:** breath
The Hebrew word translated as “breath” in these verses is translated as “spirit” by a few modern versions. It is also translated as “wind” in [Ezekiel 37:9](../37/09.md).

---

## Ezekiel 37:6

**Quote:** sinews
the tissue that connects muscles to bones

**Quote:** know that I am Yahweh
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-explicit
When Yahweh says that people will know that he is Yahweh, he is implying that they will know that he is the one true God who has supreme authority and power. See how you translated this in [Ezekiel 6:7](../06/07.md). Alternate translation: [understand that I am Yahweh, the one true God] or [realize that I, Yahweh, have supreme power and authority]

## Ezekiel 37:7

# General Information:

Ezekiel is speaking.

**Quote:** as I was commanded
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-activepassive
If your language does not use the passive form in this way, you can state this in active form or in another way that is natural in your language. Alternate translation: [as Yahweh had commanded me to speak]

**Quote:** behold
The word “behold” here shows that Ezekiel was surprised by what he saw.

## Ezekiel 37:8

This refers to the parts of human bodies that are like hard strings and hold the bones and muscles together. See how you translated this in [Ezekiel 37:6](../37/06.md).

**Quote:** But there was still no breath in them
The noun “breath” can be stated as the verb “breathing.” Alternate translation: [But they were not breathing] or [But they were not alive]

## Ezekiel 37:11

**Quote:** the entire house of Israel
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metonymy
The word “house” is a metonym for the family that lives in the house, in this case the Israelites, the descendants of Jacob over many years. See how you translated this in [Ezekiel 3:1](../03/01.md).

**Quote:** Behold!
The word “Behold” here adds emphasis to what follows. Alternate translation: [Indeed!]

**Quote:** We have been cut off
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
The people being destroyed and removed from their land is spoken of as if they had been cut off like a person would cut off a branch or piece of cloth. Alternate translation: [Our nation is destroyed]

---

## Ezekiel 37:12

**Quote:** I will open your graves and lift you out from them
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Yahweh restoring the people and bringing them back to their land is spoken of as if he will bring them back to life again.

## Ezekiel 37:15

**Quote:** the word of Yahweh came
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-idiom
This is an idiom that is used to introduce something that God told his prophets or his people. See how you translated this in [Ezekiel 3:16](../03/16.md). Alternate translation: [Yahweh spoke this message] or [Yahweh spoke these words]

## Ezekiel 37:16

**Quote:** For Judah
Here “For” means “representing.” Alternate translation: [Representing Judah] or [Belonging to Judah]

**Quote:** Judah
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-synecdoche
The tribe of Judah lived in the southern kingdom of Israel which was called Judah. Here the name is used to refer to the whole southern kingdom.

**Quote:** the people of Israel, his companions
This refers to the Israelites who lived in the southern kingdom of Judah. Alternate translation: [all the tribes of Judah] or [the tribes of Israel who are part of the kingdom of Judah]

**Quote:** For Joseph, the branch of Ephraim
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-synecdoche
Joseph was Ephraim’s father. The tribe of Ephraim lived in the northern kingdom of Israel. Here the names are used to represent the whole northern kingdom.

**Quote:** all the people of Israel, their companions
This refers to the Israelites who lived in the northern kingdom of Israel. Alternate translation: [all the tribes of Israel] or [the Israelites who were part of the kingdom of Israel]

“the stick of Joseph.” This represents the kingdom of Israel.

**Quote:** that is in the hand of Ephraim

**Quote:** the tribes of Israel his companions
Alternate translation: [the other tribes of Israel who are his companions] or [the other tribes of Israel who are part of that kingdom]

**Quote:** the branch of Judah
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metonymy
“the stick of Judah.” This represents the kingdom of Judah.

**Quote:** over them
Alternate translation: [over the people of Israel]

**Quote:** will walk according to my decrees
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
This speaks of acting or behaving in a certain way as if it were a person walking. Alternate translation: [will live as I have commanded]

## Ezekiel 37:26

# General Information:

**Quote:** I will establish
Alternate translation: [I will set up] or [I will create]

“a covenant that brings peace.” See how you translated this in [Ezekiel 34:25](../34/25.md).

**Quote:** I will establish them
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-explicit
It was implied that Yahweh would establish them in the land of Israel. Alternate translation: [I will set them in the land of Israel] or [I will cause them to live securely in the land of Israel]

**Quote:** multiply them
Alternate translation: [cause their people to increase in number]

**Quote:** my holy place
Alternate translation: [my holy sanctuary] or [my holy temple]

**Quote:** in their midst
Alternate translation: [among them]
