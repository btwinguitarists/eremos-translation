## Chapter intro

# Ezekiel 46 General Notes

## Structure and Formatting

Instructions for the new temple and city continue in this chapter. This chapter focuses on many of the temple’s rules. (See: [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/temple]])

---

## Ezekiel 46:1

**Quote:** The Lord Yahweh says this
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-123person
Yahweh speaks of himself by name to remind Ezekiel and the people that what he is saying is important. See how you translated this in [Ezekiel 6:11](../06/11.md). Alternate translation: [I, the Lord Yahweh, say this]

**Quote:** The gate of the inner courtyard, facing east
Alternate translation: [the gate on the east side of the inner courtyard] or [the eastern gate of the inner courtyard]

**Quote:** inner courtyard
See how you translated this in [Ezekiel 8:16](../08/16.md).

## Ezekiel 46:12

**Quote:** the gate facing east will be opened for him
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-activepassive
If your language does not use the passive form in this way, you can state this in active form or in another way that is natural in your language. Alternate translation: [the Levites will open the gate facing east for him]

**Quote:** the gate facing east
Alternate translation: [the eastern gate] or [the gate on the east side of the courtyard]
