# Ezekiel 8 General Notes

## Religious and Cultural Concepts in This Chapter

### Judah’s sin

The people have contaminated the temple with their worship of other gods and through their sin. (See: [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/temple]] and [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/falsegod]] and [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/sin]])

## Ezekiel 8:1

# General Information:

Ezekiel tells about another vision he saw.

**Quote:** So it came about
This phrase is used here to mark the beginning of a new part of the story. If your language has a way for doing this, you could consider using it here.

**Quote:** in the sixth year
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/translate-ordinal
You may need to make explicit the time period of which this is the sixth year. Alternate translation: [in the sixth year of the exile of King Jehoiachin] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-explicit]])

**Quote:** the sixth month, in the fifth day of the month
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/translate-hebrewmonths
This is the sixth month of the Hebrew calendar. The fifth day is near the beginning of September on Western calendars. Alternate translation: [the fifth day of the sixth month] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/translate-ordinal]])

**Quote:** the hand of the Lord Yahweh again fell upon me
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
This should be translated literally, as Ezekiel later sees something like a hand. Others may choose to consider the hand a metaphor for Yahweh’s presence or power.

**Quote:** Lord Yahweh
See how you translated this in [Ezekiel 2:4](../02/04.md).

**Quote:** fell upon
Alternate translation: [took hold of]

## Ezekiel 8:2

**Quote:** a likeness with the appearance of a man
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-abstractnouns
Here, **likeness** means that what Ezekiel saw looked like a man. If your language does not use an abstract noun for the ideas behind the words **likeness** and **appearance**, you can express the same ideas with verbal forms. Alternate translation: [there was someone who appeared to be a man]

**Quote:** glowing metal
When metal is very hot, it glows with a yellow or orange light.

**Quote:** the appearance of his hips … the appearance of something shining
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-abstractnouns
If your language does not use an abstract noun for the idea behind the word **appearance**, you can express the same idea with a verbal form. Alternate translation: [what appeared to be his hips … what appeared to be something shining]

## Ezekiel 8:3

**Quote:** he reached out
The word “he” probably refers to the “figure like a man” ([Ezekiel 8:2](../08/02.md)).

**Quote:** between earth and heaven
Alternate translation: [between the ground and the sky]

**Quote:** in visions from God, he brought me to Jerusalem
The words “in visions” mean that this experience is happening in Ezekiel’s thoughts. His body would still be in his home while God shows him these things.

**Quote:** the inner northern gate
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-explicit
“the inner northern gate of the temple.” The temple was surrounded by two walls, one inside the other. This gate was on the north side of the inner wall.

**Quote:** the idol that provokes great jealousy
Alternate translation: [the idol that causes God to be very jealous]

## Ezekiel 8:4

**Quote:** according to the vision I had seen on the plain
This could mean: (1) “who looked the same as what I had seen in the vision that I saw when I was on the plain” or (2) “who looked the same as what I saw when I was on the plain.”

**Quote:** the plain
a large area of flat land that has few trees.

## Ezekiel 8:5

# General Information:

The “figure like a man” ([Ezekiel 8:2](../08/02.md)) speaks to Ezekiel.

**Quote:** Son of man
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-explicit
“Son of a human being” or “Son of humanity.” God calls Ezekiel this to emphasize that Ezekiel is only a human being. God is eternal and powerful, but humans are not. See how you translated this in [Ezekiel 2:1](../02/01.md). Alternate translation: [Mortal person] or [Human]

**Quote:** lift up your eyes … lifted up my eyes
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-idiom
This idiom means that he should look towards something. Alternate translation: [look up … looked up] or [turn your head and look … turned my head and looked]

**Quote:** gate leading to the altar
Alternate translation: [gate through which people would walk so they could go to the altar]

## Ezekiel 8:6

**Quote:** do you see what they are doing?
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-rquestion
God uses this question to bring Ezekiel’s attention to what the people were doing. Alternate translation: [I want you to understand why I hate what the people here are doing.]

**Quote:** the house of Israel
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metonymy
The word “house” is a metonym for the family that lives in the house, in this case the Israelites, the descendants of Jacob over many years. See how you translated this in [Ezekiel 3:1](../03/01.md). Alternate translation: [the Israelites] or [the Israelite people group]

## Ezekiel 8:17

**Quote:** Do you see this … man?
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-rquestion
Yahweh is commanding Ezekiel to think about what he has just seen. Alternate translation: [Think about this … man.]

**Quote:** Is it a little thing for the house of Judah to do these abominations that they are doing here?
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-rquestion
God uses this question to show that he is right to be angry at the people of Judah. Alternate translation: [I am right to be angry at the house of Judah because of these abominations that they are doing here.]

**Quote:** house of Judah
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metonymy
The word “house” is a metonym for the family that lives in the house, in this case the descendants of Judah over many years. See how you translated these words in [Ezekiel 3:1](../03/01.md). Alternate translation: [the Judah people group] or [the people of Judah]

**Quote:** they have filled the land with violence
Alternate translation: [throughout the country they are doing violent things] or [all over the country they are attacking one another]

**Quote:** to provoke me to anger
Alternate translation: [to make me angry]

**Quote:** putting the branch to their noses
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-idiom
This could mean: (1) the people were using the branches in false worship or (2) the people were using the branches to show rebellion against Yahweh. The words “branch to … noses” may be a literal tree branch and literal noses, or they could be the term for a hand gesture.

## Ezekiel 8:18

**Quote:** I will not spare them
Alternate translation: [I will still punish them]

**Quote:** Though they cry in my ears with a loud voice
Alternate translation: [Though they yell their prayers to me with a loud voice]

**Quote:** I will not hear them
