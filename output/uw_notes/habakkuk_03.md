## Chapter intro

# Habakkuk 3 Introduction

## Structure and Formatting

This chapter is a prayer-psalm (verse 1) describing a theophany, Yahweh’s appearance as a divine warrior. It includes a superscription (verse 1), a liturgical colophon (end of verse 19), and three Selah markers, indicating it was used in temple worship.

## Religious and Cultural Concepts in This Chapter

### Theophany

Verses 3-15 describe God coming from Teman with cosmic power, shaking mountains, splitting rivers, and commanding the sun and moon. These images draw on the tradition of Yahweh’s [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/save]] at the Red Sea.

### From Terror to Trust

The chapter pivots at verse 16: Habakkuk trembles with fear at God’s power, yet resolves to wait quietly and rejoice even when all provision fails (verses 17-19).
