## Chapter intro

# Isaiah 24 General Notes

## Structure and Formatting

Some translations set each line of poetry farther to the right than the rest of the text to make it easier to read. The ULT does this with the poetry in this chapter.

While it appears that this chapter is a continuation of the prophecy from the previous chapter, it actually looks at the whole of the Assyrian conquest as Yahweh’s punishment on the world. (See: [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/prophet]])

## Religious and Cultural Concepts in This Chapter

### Covenant

The mention of an “everlasting covenant” in this chapter is probably not to a specific covenant with Abraham or Moses. Instead, it is an expectation that mankind would obey Yahweh. Some consider this to be a covenant Yahweh made with Adam. (See: [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/eternity]] and [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/covenant]])

## Translation Issues in This Chapter

### Reign of Yahweh

This chapter appears to prophesy about a time of restoration when the Messiah will reign. This chapter pictures a time of great peace and harmony in the world. The translator should not add explanation, but should try to maintain the tense of the original text, as a future or uncompleted action. (See: [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/restore]] and [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/prophet]] and [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/christ]])

## Isaiah 24:2

**Quote:** It will come about that
This phrase marks an important event. If your language has a way for doing this, you could consider using it here.

**Quote:** as with … so with
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-ellipsis
What Yahweh will do is not stated here, but it is understood. This shows that God will treat all people the same way. Alternate translation: [as Yahweh scatters … so he will scatter]

**Quote:** the priest … the giver of interest
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-genericnoun
In [24:2](../24/02.md) Isaiah lists several classes of people. They can be expressed as plural nouns as in the UST. Alternate translation: [the priests … those who give interest]

**Quote:** the receiver of interest
“the one who owes money.” The word “interest” means the extra money someone has to pay so that he may borrow money.

**Quote:** the giver of interest
Alternate translation: [the one who is owed money]

## Isaiah 24:10

**Quote:** The city of chaos has been broken down
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-activepassive
If your language does not use the passive form in this way, you can state this in active form or in another way that is natural in your language. Alternate translation: [Yahweh will break down the city of chaos]

**Quote:** The city of chaos
This could mean: (1) the city was in chaos before the Lord breaks it down; the people did not obey God, the government was corrupt, and the city was full of drinking and parties, or (2) the city will be in chaos after God breaks it down. The walls and buildings that were once built strong and tall are now in ruins on the ground. With either meaning it does not mean one city. It refers to cities in general.

**Quote:** every house is closed up and empty
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-activepassive
If your language does not use the passive form in this way, you can state this in active form or in another way that is natural in your language. Alternate translation: [the people will close up their houses and leave them empty]

## Isaiah 24:13

**Quote:** as when an olive tree is beaten, as the gleanings when the grape harvest is done
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-simile
This compares the nations after Yahweh devastates the land to trees and vines after their fruit has been picked. This means there will be very few people left in the land.

## Isaiah 24:14

**Quote:** They will lift up their voices and shout the majesty of Yahweh
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-idiom
The phrase “lift up their voices” is an idiom that means to speak loudly. Alternate translation: [They will sing and shout about the majesty of Yahweh]

**Quote:** They will
Here “They” refers to those who are still alive after Yahweh devastates the earth.

**Quote:** and will joyfully shout from the sea
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-explicit
Here “the sea” refers to the Mediterranean Sea which is west of Israel. Alternate translation: [and those in the west towards the sea will shout with joy]

## Isaiah 24:15

**Quote:** Therefore in the east glorify Yahweh
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metonymy
The phrase “in the east” represents the people living east of Israel. Isaiah is commanding these people as if they were there with him. But, he is talking to the people in the future after God devastates the earth. Alternate translation: [Therefore everyone from distant lands in the east will glorify Yahweh] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-apostrophe]] and [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-pastforfuture]])

**Quote:** in the isles of the sea give glory
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metonymy
Isaiah is commanding the people who live on the islands in the Mediterranean sea as if they were there with him. But, he is talking to the people in the future after God devastates the earth. Alternate translation: [everyone in the islands will give glory] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-apostrophe]] and [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-pastforfuture]])

**Quote:** to the name of Yahweh
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metonymy
Here “name” represents Yahweh. Alternate translation: [to Yahweh]

## Isaiah 24:16

**Quote:** we have heard
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-exclusive
Here “we” refers to Isaiah and the people of Israel. Isaiah is describing something in the future as if it has already happened. Alternate translation: [we will hear] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-pastforfuture]])

**Quote:** I have wasted away, I have wasted away
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-parallelism
Isaiah repeats this phrase to emphasize his anguish. He was very concerned because he saw people who deceived others and did not do what they promised to do. Alternate translation: [I have become very weak]

**Quote:** The treacherous have dealt treacherously; yes, the treacherous have dealt very treacherously
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-parallelism
Isaiah repeats this phrase to emphasize his anguish. Alternate translation: [Indeed, those who deceive are now deceiving others] or [Indeed, the deceivers have acted deceitfully]

## Isaiah 24:17

**Quote:** Terror, the pit, and the snare are upon you, inhabitants of the earth
Alternate translation: [You people of the earth will experience terror, the pit, and the snare]

**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-synecdoche
Here “pit” and “snare” represent all the different bad things that will happen to people. People will run away to escape one bad thing but they will just experience another bad thing.

## Isaiah 24:18

**Quote:** caught in the snare
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-synecdoche
Here the “snare” represents all the different bad things that will happen to people. People will run away to escape one bad thing but they will just experience another bad thing.

**Quote:** the sound of terror
Alternate translation: [the terrifying sound]

**Quote:** will be caught in the snare
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-activepassive
If your language does not use the passive form in this way, you can state this in active form or in another way that is natural in your language. Alternate translation: [the snare will catch him]

**Quote:** The windows of the heavens will be opened
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
This speaks of large amounts of rain falling from the sky as if Yahweh opened a window in the sky and let the water pour through. Alternate translation: [The sky will split open and torrents of rain will fall]

**Quote:** the foundations of the earth will shake
The word “foundation” normally refers to a stone structure that gives support to a building from underneath. Here it describes a similar structure that was thought to support and hold the earth in place. Isaiah says even the structure that holds the earth will shake. Alternate translation: [the earth will shake terribly] or [there will be a terrible earthquake]

## Isaiah 24:20

**Quote:** The earth will stagger like a drunken man and it will sway back and forth like a hut
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-simile
These similes emphasize how the earth will shake back and forth.

**Quote:** Its sin will be heavy on it and it will fall and never rise again
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
This speaks of the earth as if it were a person and transgressions were a heavy object. The person tries to carry the heavy weight but the weight causes the person to fall and not be able to stand back up. Here the earth represents the people of the earth who cause Yahweh to destroy the earth because of their sins. Alternate translation: [The sins of the people are many and so Yahweh will destroy the earth, and the earth will be like a person who falls and cannot get back up] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metonymy]])

## Isaiah 24:23

**Quote:** Then the moon will be ashamed, and the sun disgraced
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-personification
