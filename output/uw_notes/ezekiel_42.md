## Chapter intro

# Ezekiel 42 General Notes

## Structure and Formatting

Instructions for the new temple and city continue in this chapter. This chapter focused on the storerooms for keeping the holy things of the temple. (See: [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/temple]] and [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/holy]])

## Ezekiel 42:2

**Quote:** one hundred cubits … fifty cubits
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/translate-bdistance
Each long cubit was about 54 centimeters. See how you translated this in [Ezekiel 40:5](../40/05.md). Alternate translation: [100 cubits … 50 cubits] or [about 54 meters … about 27 meters]
