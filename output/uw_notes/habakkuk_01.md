## Chapter intro

# Habakkuk 1 Introduction

## Structure and Formatting

This chapter is a prophetic lament dialogue: the prophet complains about injustice, Yahweh announces the Chaldean invasion, then the prophet protests again. See: [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/lament]]

## Religious and Cultural Concepts in This Chapter

### The Oracle (Burden)

The Hebrew word for “burden” also means a prophetic oracle. Translators may need to use “message” or “oracle” rather than a physical load.

### Justice

Habakkuk protests that [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/justice]] has collapsed in Judah, then questions why Yahweh uses an even more wicked nation to punish them.
