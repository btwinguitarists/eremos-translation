## Chapter intro

# Isaiah 59 General Notes

## Structure and Formatting

Some translations set each line of poetry farther to the right than the rest of the text to make it easier to read. The ULT does this with the poetry in this chapter.

## Religious and Cultural Concepts in This Chapter

### Yahweh’s deliverance

When Israel was conquered, they were being punished for their sin. Yahweh is strong enough to deliver them, but he did not because he desired to refine them through the experience of the exile. (See: [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/other/deliverer]] and [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/sin]])

---

## Isaiah 59:1

**Quote:** Look
“Behold!” or “You should know!” Yahweh tells the people to pay attention.

**Quote:** Yahweh’s hand is not so short
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metonymy
“Hand” represents power and ability. A “short” hand does not have power and ability. Alternate translation: [Yahweh is fully able] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor]])

---

## Isaiah 59:2

**Quote:** your … you
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-you
These plural pronouns refer to the people of Israel as a single group.

**Quote:** your sins have made him hide his face from you
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-personification
“Face” represents being present and looking upon. Alternate translation: [your sins have made him turn away from you]

---

## Isaiah 59:3

**Quote:** For your hands are stained with blood and your fingers with sin
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metonymy
Here “hands” and “fingers” refers to their actions. This means they are guilty of doing violent and sinful things. “Your” is plural. Alternate translation: [For you have committed violent sins] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-you]])

**Quote:** Your lips speak lies and your tongue speaks maliciously
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metonymy
The parts of the body that make speech represent what people say. Alternate translation: [You speak lies and malicious things]

---

## Isaiah 59:4

**Quote:** they conceive trouble and give birth to sin
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
“Conceiving” and “giving birth” emphasize how carefully they plan to do sinful things. Here “they” still refers to the people of Israel. Alternate translation: [they work hard to do sinful things]

---

## Isaiah 59:5

**Quote:** They hatch eggs of a poisonous snake
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Eggs of a poisonous snake hatch into more dangerous snakes. “Poisonous snakes” represents evil the people do that harms more and more. Alternate translation: [They make evil that spreads out to make more evil]

**Quote:** weave a spider’s web
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
This represents the deeds of the people that are useless. Alternate translation: [produce things and activities that are useless]

**Quote:** Whoever eats of their eggs dies, and if an egg is crushed, it hatches into a poisonous snake
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Eating a poisonous egg will kill the one that eats it and represents self-destruction. Breaking the egg allows the young poisonous snake to hatch and represents spreading destruction. Alternate translation: [The activities they do will destroy them and will spread destruction to others]

**Quote:** if an egg is crushed
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-activepassive
If your language does not use the passive form in this way, you can state this in active form or in another way that is natural in your language. Alternate translation: [if someone crushes an egg]

---

## Isaiah 59:6

**Quote:** Their webs cannot be used for garments, nor can they cover themselves with their works
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
This means their sinful deeds cannot be covered and hidden, just as a web cannot serve as clothing and cover someone. Alternate translation: [Their evil deeds will be exposed as useless]

**Quote:** Their webs cannot be used for garments
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-activepassive
If your language does not use the passive form in this way, you can state this in active form or in another way that is natural in your language. Alternate translation: [They cannot clothe themselves with their webs]

**Quote:** deeds of violence are in their hands
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metonymy
“Hands” represents the ability and power to do these things and therefore their responsibility. Alternate translation: [they are fully responsible for the violence they do]

---

## Isaiah 59:7

**Quote:** Their feet run to evil
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-synecdoche
Here people are represented by their “feet.” This speaks of them doing something quickly as their feet running to it. Alternate translation: [They are quick to do evil things] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor]])

**Quote:** are their roads
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
“roads” represents their way of life. Alternate translation: [are all they do]

---

## Isaiah 59:8

**Quote:** there is no justice in their paths
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
“paths” represents their way of life. Alternate translation: [they never do what is just] or [everything they do is unjust]

**Quote:** They have made crooked paths
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
“Crooked paths” represents the way of life that is corrupt. Alternate translation: [They say and do dishonest things. They are devious]

---

## Isaiah 59:9

**Quote:** justice is far from us
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-exclusive
Here “us” refers to Isaiah and the people of Israel. “Far” represents that justice is gone and difficult to get. Alternate translation: [justice is gone and very difficult to get] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor]])

**Quote:** We wait for light, but see darkness; we look for brightness, but we walk in darkness
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-parallelism
Each of these phrases means that the people are waiting for God’s goodness, but it seems like he has abandoned them. (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor]])

---

## Isaiah 59:10

**Quote:** We grope for the wall like the blind … like dead men
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-simile
This means that because God is not coming to them, they feel helpless, not finding the right path and despairing of the future, with no hope for a vibrant life.

---

## Isaiah 59:11

**Quote:** We growl like bears and moan like doves
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-simile
These refer to the sounds the people made because they were distressed and mourning.

---

## Isaiah 59:12

**Quote:** our many transgressions
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-exclusive
Here “our” refers to Isaiah and the people of Israel.

**Quote:** before you
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-you
Here “you” refers to Yahweh.

**Quote:** our sins testify against us
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-personification
Isaiah describes the sins as a person who goes before God to declare that the people are guilty.

**Quote:** for our transgressions are with us
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
“With us” represents being aware of them. Alternate translation: [for we are aware of our transgressions]

---

## Isaiah 59:14

**Quote:** Justice is driven back
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-activepassive
If your language does not use the passive form in this way, you can state this in active form or in another way that is natural in your language. Alternate translation: [The people drive justice back]

**Quote:** Justice … righteousness … truth … right
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-personification
These are ideas that Isaiah describes as acting like persons. (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-abstractnouns]])

---

## Isaiah 59:15

**Quote:** Trustworthiness
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-personification
Isaiah describes this idea as acting like a person. (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-abstractnouns]])

---

## Isaiah 59:16

**Quote:** He saw that there was no man, and wondered that there was no one to intervene.
Alternate translation: [Yahweh was appalled that no one came to help those who were suffering.] or [Yahweh was astonished that no one came to help those who were suffering.]

**Quote:** Therefore his own arm brought salvation for him
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metonymy
Yahweh’s “arm” represents his ability and power. Alternate translation: [Yahweh used his own power to save the people]

**Quote:** his righteousness sustained him
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-personification
“Righteousness” here is a quality that acts like a person. The word can be translated as an adjective. Alternate translation: [he did right as he always would do] (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-abstractnouns]])

---

## Isaiah 59:17

**Quote:** He put on righteousness as a breastplate and a helmet of salvation upon his head. He clothed himself with garments of vengeance and wore zeal as a mantle
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
“breastplate,” “helmet,” “garments,” and “mantle” are clothing for war and fighting. Isaiah describes Yahweh as putting these on to punish his people. (See also: [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-simile]])

**Quote:** mantle
a loose, flowing robe

---

## Isaiah 59:18

**Quote:** He repaid them
Isaiah is describing a future event as if it happened in the past. This means that it will certainly happen.

---

## Isaiah 59:19

**Quote:** fear the name of Yahweh
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metonymy
Here “name” refers to Yahweh’s reputation and character. Alternate translation: [fear Yahweh]

**Quote:** from the west … from the sun’s rising
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-merism
Isaiah combines these words to mean people in all places of the world.

**Quote:** for he will come as a rushing stream
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-simile
Narrow valleys in Judah were dry most of the year until a sudden, heavy rain turned them into fast-moving water. When that happened there was a lot of noise and wind.

**Quote:** driven by the breath of Yahweh
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-activepassive
If your language does not use the passive form in this way, you can state this in active form or in another way that is natural in your language. Alternate translation: [which the breath of Yahweh drives]

---

## Isaiah 59:20

**Quote:** this is Yahweh’s declaration
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-123person
Yahweh speaks of himself by name to express the certainty of what he is declaring. Translate as in [Isaiah 30:1](../30/01.md). Alternate translation: [this is what Yahweh has declared] or [this is what I, Yahweh, have declared]

---

## Isaiah 59:21

**Quote:** my words which I have put in your mouth
Alternate translation: [the message I have given you to speak]
