## Chapter intro

# Habakkuk 2 Introduction

## Structure and Formatting

This chapter contains Yahweh’s answer to Habakkuk’s second complaint. After instructing the prophet to write down a [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/other/vision]], Yahweh pronounces five [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/woe]] oracles against the oppressor (verses 6-20), each beginning with “Woe.”

## Religious and Cultural Concepts in This Chapter

### The Righteous Shall Live by Faith

Verse 4 contrasts the proud, whose soul is not upright, with the [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/righteous]], who shall live by [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/faith]]. This verse is quoted three times in the New Testament.

### Idolatry

The final woe oracle (verses 18-20) condemns the making and worshiping of idols, contrasting them with Yahweh, who is in his holy temple.
