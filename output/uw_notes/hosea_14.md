## Chapter intro

# Hosea 14 General Notes

## Structure and Formatting

This last chapter of Hosea ends with hope. In this chapter, Hosea calls for Israel to confess to Yahweh and repent. This chapter continues to be written in poetic form, using metaphors and other devices to communicate God’s love for his sinful people. (See: [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/confess]], [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/repent]], [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/love]], [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/sin]] and [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/peopleofgod]] and [[rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor]])

Why are confession and repentance even suggested here for a people that Yahweh has already condemned to be destroyed? It is because this is a teaching that Yahweh is a merciful God who loves his people. (See: [[rc://*/tw/dict/bible/kt/mercy]])

## Translation Issues in This Chapter

### Speaker

The use of first and second person in this chapter often signifies a change in speaker. In this chapter, Hosea is speaking to the northern kingdom to tell them that Yahweh loves them.
There are several places in this chapter where the writer switches from the third person to the second or the first person. This may cause some confusion about who is speaking.

# General Information:

Hosea the prophet is speaking.

**Quote:** for you have fallen because of your iniquity

## Hosea 14:2

**Quote:** Take with you words
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-explicit
This probably means words of confession and praise. Alternate translation: [Confess your sins]

---

## Hosea 14:3

# General Information:

Hosea is speaking

**Quote:** to the work of our hands
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-synecdoche
Here people are represented by their “hands” to refer to the people making things. Alternate translation: [to the idols we made]

**Quote:** the fatherless person
This expression stands perhaps for the entire nation. It indicates God’s enormous compassion for his people.

**Quote:** finds compassion
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-abstractnouns
If your language does not use an abstract noun for the idea behind the word **compassion**, you can express the same idea with an adjective. Alternate translation: [finds one who is compassionate]

## Hosea 14:4

# General Information:

Yahweh is speaking.

**Quote:** I will heal their turning away
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Stopping the people from turning away from God is spoken of as if he were healing them.

**Quote:** their turning away
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
The failure of the people to obey God is spoken of as if they had physically turned away from him.

---

## Hosea 14:5

**Quote:** I will be like the dew to Israel; he will blossom like the lily
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-simile
God is spoken of as if he were dew that brought needed moisture to plants, and Israel is spoken of as if it were one person, and as if he were a flower that could blossom.

**Quote:** take root like a cedar in Lebanon
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-simile
The picture of Israel in the form of a plant is continued here, but this time in the form of a tall cedar tree in Lebanon, which was known for such trees.

---

## Hosea 14:6

**Quote:** His branches will spread out … like the cedars in Lebanon
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-simile
This passage continues the same image.

## Hosea 14:7

# General Information:

Yahweh is speaking.

**Quote:** The people who live in his shade will return
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
The people of Israel will once again live protected by God. Israel is spoken of as living in his shade.

**Quote:** they will revive like grain and blossom like vines
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-simile
Israel’s new prosperity is spoken of in agricultural terms.

**Quote:** His fame will be like the wine of Lebanon
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-simile
Just as Lebanon’s wine was famous, so Israel will be famous. Alternate translation: [People everywhere will know about the nation of Israel]

---

## Hosea 14:8

**Quote:** what more have I to do with idols?
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-rquestion
This passage signifies that God will stop the people of Israel from worshiping idols any longer.

**Quote:** a cypress whose leaves are always green
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-simile
A cypress is a tree whose leaves stay green all year. This represents Yahweh and his blessings on Israel.

**Quote:** from me comes your fruit
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Here “fruit” represents every good thing that comes from Yahweh.

## Hosea 14:9

# General Information:

Hosea the prophet is speaking.

**Quote:** Who is wise that he may understand these things? Who understands these things so that he may know them?
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-rquestion
The prophet uses these questions to say that wise people will understand and listen to what has been said to them.

**Quote:** For the ways of Yahweh are right, and the righteous will walk in them
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Yahweh’s commandments are spoken of as ways to walk in.

**Quote:** stumble
**Support:** rc://*/ta/man/translate/figs-metaphor
Disobeying Yahweh is spoken of as if it were stumbling while walking.
